About GradeClash

Making family game night smarter, faster, and completely free.

GradeClash: Kids vs. Parents Trivia is a free, browser-based family trivia board game created for US families, parents, teachers, and students in Grades 1 through 8. Our mission is simple: turn quick 3-to-5-minute breaks into fun, screen-positive family battles where everyone has a fair shot at winning.

Built for Families, Not Just Kids: GradeClash is designed as a shared family entertainment experience. Parents and older players face tougher general-knowledge and upper-grade questions, while students tackle age-appropriate US curriculum questions in Math, Science, History, and English Language Arts.

Why Families Love GradeClash

  • Fair-Play Handicap Mode: Kids answer questions tailored to their selected grade (Grades 1–8), while parents get harder trivia challenges so matches stay exciting down to the final tile.
  • Zero Login & Zero Downloads: No accounts, no passwords, and no app-store clutter. Open GradeClash.com on any phone, tablet, or laptop and play instantly.
  • Daily Challenge & Streaks: Every day brings a fresh, shared Daily Challenge board so families can compare scores and keep their winning streak alive.
  • Solo Mode for Quick Warm-Ups: Students can also play solo against our built-in computer challenger to sharpen their skills before challenging Mom or Dad.

Our Commitment to Safe Play

We respect family privacy. GradeClash does not require registration, does not collect personal profiles, and stores your optional Daily Streak strictly inside your own browser's local storage.
