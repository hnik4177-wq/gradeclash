Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Welcome to GradeClash.com ("we," "our," or "us"). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit and play games on https://gradeclash.com.

1. No Account or Personal Information Required

GradeClash is a zero-login web game. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, home address, or payment information to play. If you voluntarily contact us via email, we use your email address solely to respond to your inquiry.

2. Local Browser Storage (localStorage)

To save your Daily Challenge completion status and winning streak on your device, GradeClash uses your browser's built-in localStorage. This data stays strictly on your own device and is never transmitted to our servers. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

3. Advertising, Cookies & Third-Party Partners (Ezoic / Google)

To keep GradeClash 100% free for families, we may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks, including Google Ad Manager / Google AdSense and Ezoic.

  • Third-party vendors use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and limit how often you see the same ad.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

4. Family & Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

GradeClash is a general-audience family trivia game designed for parents and students to enjoy together. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information (PII) from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe any personal information has been submitted to us via email, please contact us at contact@gradeclash.com and we will promptly delete it.

5. Third-Party Links

Our victory screen includes optional buttons to share your score on external platforms such as WhatsApp or Facebook. When you click those buttons, you leave GradeClash.com and are subject to the privacy policies of those platforms.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at contact@gradeclash.com.
