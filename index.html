How to Play GradeClash (🇺🇸 US Grades 1–8)

GradeClash is a free online kids vs. parents trivia board game built for US families and students in Grades 1 through 8. Pick your hero avatar and grade above, hit Start Match, and race inward along the 25-tile spiral from Start (0) to the Trophy Center (24).

  • Dual-Side Controls: Parents roll from the top dock and Kids roll from the bottom dock, each with their own live score, dice, and ✂️ 50-50 Lifeline.
  • Fair-Play Handicap: Students answer age-appropriate US curriculum questions (Math, Science, History, ELA), while parents face tougher general-knowledge trivia.
  • Special Board Twists: Jump +2 on ⭐ Bonus Stars, dodge 🍌 Slippery Detours (-2), knock opponents back when landing on their tile, and trigger wild twists on ❓ Mystery Tile 13.
  • Daily Challenge & Streaks: Switch to the Daily Challenge tab to play today's shared global board and build your 🔥 Daily Streak.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GradeClash free and safe for families? Yes! GradeClash is 100% free in any mobile or desktop browser with no downloads, no sign-ups, and no personal accounts required.
What subjects are covered in Grades 1–8? Every match mixes US Elementary and Middle School Math, Science, US History & Geography, and English Language Arts (ELA).
Can kids practice alone? Yes! Choose Solo Mode to play a quick warm-up match against our built-in Computer Challenger.
