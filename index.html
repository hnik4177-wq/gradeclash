GradeClash: Free Kids vs. Parents Trivia Board Game (US Grades 1–8)

Looking for a fast, fun, and screen-positive game for family night? GradeClash is a free online kids vs. parents trivia game built for US families and students in Grades 1 through 8. Designed to fit a 3-to-5-minute match, GradeClash combines classic spiral board-race excitement with real US school curriculum trivia in Math, Science, History, Geography, and English Language Arts.

How to Play the Spiral Board Race

  • Choose Your Mode: Play Pass & Play together on one phone or tablet, or pick Solo Mode to warm up against our built-in computer challenger.
  • Fair-Play Handicap: In Kids vs. Parents mode, students answer questions tailored to their selected grade (Grades 1–8), while parents face tougher upper-grade and general-knowledge trivia.
  • Roll, Answer & Hop: Roll the dice and answer within 15 seconds to hop inward along the 25-tile spiral from Start (0) to the Trophy Center (24).
  • Use Your ✂️ 50-50 Lifeline: Each player gets one 50-50 lifeline per match to eliminate two wrong choices on a tough question.
  • Watch for Special Tiles: Land on ⭐ Bonus Stars (6 & 17) to jump +2 tiles, dodge 🍌 Slippery Detours (10 & 20) (-2 tiles), and trigger wild twists on the glowing ❓ Mystery Tile (13)—including Rocket Boost, Push Back, or a full Position Swap!

Daily Challenge & Streak Tracking

Switch to the 📅 Daily Challenge tab to play today's fixed global board. Every player gets the exact same sequence of questions for that day. Win or finish to build your 🔥 Daily Streak (saved safely in your browser with zero login required) and share your Wordle-style result card with friends and family.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GradeClash completely free and safe for families? Yes! GradeClash is 100% free to play in any mobile or desktop browser with no downloads, no sign-ups, and no personal accounts required.
What subjects are included in the Grade 1–8 trivia bank? Questions cover US Elementary and Middle School Math, Earth & Life Science, US History & Geography, and English Grammar/Vocabulary.
How does Solo Mode work? In Solo Mode, you race against our automated Computer Challenger, making it ideal for quick solo trivia warm-ups before challenging Mom or Dad.
What happens if two players land on the same tile? If you land directly on your opponent's tile (anywhere except Start Tile 0), you knock them back 2 tiles!
